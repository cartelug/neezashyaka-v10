Working with NS · 08.0

Before you
write the email.

What does a typical project cost?

Brand identity from 800k UGX. Full websites from 1.5M–3M UGX. Event campaigns scoped per deliverable. Social media management from 400k UGX / month. AI film and creative direction quoted per project. We can talk numbers in the first call — no surprises after.

How fast can NS Creative ship?

A poster set in 48 hours. A full website in 2–4 weeks. A brand identity system in 3–5 weeks. A film like Sun Over Africa in 3 months. Speed depends on feedback speed — we work fast when the client does too.

Do you only work with Ugandan clients?

No. We're based in Kampala but ship for clients across East Africa and beyond. The work travels — we've shipped for SaaS founders, nonprofits, venue owners, film producers, and corporate strategists. If the brief is serious, the country doesn't matter.

Can NS Creative manage my brand long-term?

Yes — most of our work is retainer-based. We currently manage social, content, and ongoing design for SaaS, automotive, and ministry clients monthly. If you want a partner rather than a vendor, that's how we work best.

How do we start?

Email or WhatsApp. We jump on a 30-minute call, scope the brief, send a written proposal within 48 hours. If it's a fit, we sign and start. If not, we'll happily refer you to someone better suited.
